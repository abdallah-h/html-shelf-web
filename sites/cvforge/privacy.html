Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

The short version

Your CV never leaves your device. CV Forge runs entirely in your web browser. The information you type — your name, work history, education, everything — is stored only in your own browser's local storage and is never transmitted to, or stored on, our servers. We could not read your CV even if we wanted to.

What we do NOT collect

  • Your CV content (name, contact details, work history, education, skills, photo)
  • Account information — there are no accounts and no signup
  • Uploaded files — photos you add are converted in your browser and stay there

What may be collected

  • Analytics: We may use privacy-respecting analytics to count visits and understand which features are used (page views, browser type, country). This data is aggregated and does not identify you personally.
  • Advertising: This site displays ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings. See how Google uses data from partner sites.
  • Hosting logs: Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, request time) for security purposes.

Third-party services the app contacts

To work properly, the app loads a few resources from other providers. These requests share only standard technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) — never your CV content, except where noted:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — delivers the typefaces used in CV themes.
  • cdnjs / Cloudflare (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) — delivers the open-source JSZip library, loaded only when you export to Word.
  • QR code service (api.qrserver.com) — only if you turn on the QR code option, the profile link you chose to encode (e.g. your LinkedIn or GitHub URL) is sent to this service to generate the QR image.

Share links

If you use Share Link, your CV is encoded inside the link itself, after the # symbol. Browsers never send that part of a link to any server, so the CV still isn't transmitted to us — but anyone you give the link to can read the full CV, so share it only with people you trust. Photos are not included in share links.

Cookies

CV Forge itself sets no tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage solely to auto-save your CV on your own device. Third-party ad providers (Google) may set cookies as described above; where required by law, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to export or delete — your CV data is under your direct control. To erase it, clear your browser's site data for this website. For data held by Google in connection with advertising, see Google's privacy controls linked above.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above reflects the latest revision.
