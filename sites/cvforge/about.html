About CV Forge

CV Forge is a completely free online CV builder. No signup, no watermarks, no "premium" paywall blocking your download. Open the page, fill in your details, pick a theme, and download your CV — that's it.

Why we built it

Most resume builders let you do all the work, then demand payment at the download button. Job seekers — often the people who can least afford surprise fees — deserve better. CV Forge gives you the full toolkit for free, supported only by unobtrusive ads.

Features

  • 10 professional themes — from classic and minimal to modern, tech and creative styles
  • Live preview with accurate A4 page-break guides
  • Three export formats: PDF (print-perfect), Word .docx (editable), and plain text for ATS systems
  • Full customisation: colors, fonts, adjustable page margins and line spacing, drag-and-drop section ordering, custom sections, references, photo and QR code
  • Arabic / RTL support — build a right-to-left CV with one click
  • Multiple profiles — save different CV versions for different roles
  • Privacy by design — everything runs in your browser; your CV never touches a server

Who it's for

Students writing their first CV, professionals tailoring applications for different roles, and anyone in markets where paid resume tools are out of reach. It works on phones, tablets and desktops.
