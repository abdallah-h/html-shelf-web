📄 How to Save as PDF

1. Click the PDF button — a new window opens with your CV.
2. Your device's print dialog appears automatically.
3. Change Destination to "Save as PDF".
4. Set Paper size to A4.
5. Set margins to None or Default.
6. Click Save.

❓Frequently Asked Questions▼
Is this CV builder really free?

Yes. CV Forge is 100% free with no signup, no watermark and no paywall on downloads. The site is supported by unobtrusive ads.

Is my personal data safe?

Your CV never leaves your device. Everything you type is stored only in your own browser, and nothing is uploaded to our servers.

Are the CVs ATS-friendly?

Yes. The Traditional and Minimal themes use a clean single-column layout that applicant tracking systems read easily, and you can also download an ATS plain-text version.

How do I save my CV as a PDF?

Tap Download, choose PDF, then in the print dialog select “Save as PDF” as the destination and A4 as the paper size.

Can I create a CV in Arabic?

Yes. Open Colors & Fonts and set Text Direction to Right-to-Left. The preview and the PDF export both switch to a right-to-left layout.

Will my CV be saved if I close the page?

Yes. Your CV auto-saves in your browser every 30 seconds. To move it to another device, use Backup to download a file and restore it there.
