How to Write CV Bullet Points That Get Interviews (+ 40 Action Verbs)

2 min read · Updated 2026-10-01

Recruiters often spend well under a minute on a first read of a CV, and most of that time goes to your job titles and the bullet points underneath them. Weak bullets list duties. Strong bullets show results.

Duties vs achievements

A duty describes what anyone in the role was supposed to do. An achievement describes what you did and what changed because of it.

✗ Responsible for handling customer complaints.

✓ Resolved 40+ customer complaints a week, raising the team's satisfaction score from 82% to 94%.

The formula

Action verb + what you did + how + the result (ideally with a number).

You won't have a result for every line, but aim for at least one or two quantified bullets per role.

Before and after

✗ Worked on the company website.

✓ Rebuilt the company website's checkout page, cutting load time by 40% and reducing cart abandonment.

✗ Made reports for management.

✓ Automated weekly sales reports in Excel and Power BI, saving managers 6 hours of manual work each week.

✗ Trained new staff.

✓ Designed a two-week onboarding plan and trained 12 new hires, reducing their time to full productivity.

What if you don't have numbers?

Most people have more measurable results than they think. Look for:

  • Scale — how many customers, users, products, branches or team members?
  • Frequency — daily, weekly, how many per month?
  • Time or money saved — even an honest estimate ("about 5 hours a week") helps.
  • Improvement — faster, fewer errors, higher ratings, first-time-right.
  • Recognition — awards, promotions, being chosen to lead something.

Keep numbers honest. You should be able to explain every figure in an interview.

Quick rules

  • Start every bullet with a verb; drop "I" and "Responsible for".
  • Use past tense for previous jobs and present tense for your current one.
  • Give your most recent and relevant role 4–6 bullets; older roles need only 2–3.
  • Keep each bullet to one or two lines.
  • Put your most impressive bullet first in each role.
